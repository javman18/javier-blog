I've been making a game for a year.

It's about zombies. 2D, survival. I'm building it in Unity.

And today it has its first trailer.

</video>

You wake up on your couch, not knowing what time it is. You're not even wearing shoes.

There's no one left out there. Only them.

There's a day and night cycle. You search furniture for weapons, ammo and clothes. You barricade doors.

And you watch the noise. Because noise draws the zombies in.

It hasn't been easy.

It's been a challenge. Some weeks I moved forward a lot, others almost nothing.

But I've been building it **little by little**.

And seeing it all together, in a one-minute video, feels different.

Note: for now the project is called “Zombie Game”. Obviously, that won’t be its name. The game is in Spanish for now, but I’m planning to localize it to English.
